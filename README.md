## 🪴 About the Game

**Garden Guardian** is a fast, dangerously addictive arcade game inspired by the pocket LCD games
many of us grew up with. Simple rules, a couple of actions, and a score counter that climbs faster
and faster — but under the hood there's modern design: objectives, progression, unlockable content,
and a fair difficulty curve.

> Easy to pick up. Hard to put down. Even harder to beat your high score.

## ✨ Features

| | Feature | Description |
|:-:|---|---|
| ⚡ | **Dynamic difficulty** | The more points you score, the faster the game *and the music* get. Keep your cool as the pressure builds. |
| 🎯 | **Trial Mode** | 18 objective-based levels to work through one by one. |
| ♾️ | **Endless Mode** | Four score attack variants, each tougher than the last. |
| 🖼️ | **Unlockable art** | Over 50 unique illustrations earned by hitting various milestones. |
| 🏅 | **Stats & Awards** | Unlock content through stat-based milestones, plus 13 in-game Awards for even more rewards. |
| 📚 | **Teaching tools** | An in-depth tutorial stage and a series of unlockable tips covering advanced techniques. |
